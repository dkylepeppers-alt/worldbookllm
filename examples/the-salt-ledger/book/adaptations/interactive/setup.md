# Story setup

How this interactive book is discovered by playing it (worldbookllm, ADR 0033). The premise is the story kernel in story.md, the variables and what they mean are in ink/variables.md, and the rules for choices are in choice-rules.md.

## Shape

A compact, tense narrative of roughly 8-12 scenes, unfolding across two days and one night. The mood is maritime gothic by way of accounting: damp paper, lamp oil, the weight of columns that balance for the wrong reasons. Choices are not heroic or villainous but procedural — Wren must decide what to copy, what to confront, what to let pass, who to tell and how much. Varga's mentorship must remain genuinely complicated — she is not a cackling villain but someone who made a choice long ago and stuck to it, who sees Wren's potential and may even love her for finding the truth, yet cannot let the system collapse. The harbor itself is a character: the tide that comes in regardless, the plaque that remembers eleven men, berth nine where the Tidewardens keep their boats and the lamp is lit. The answers are human: debts, smuggling, promises, and grief. Nothing supernatural.
