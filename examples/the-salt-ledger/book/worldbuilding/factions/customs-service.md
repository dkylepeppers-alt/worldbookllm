# Customs Service

The government service responsible for assessing and recording the salt tithe on all cargo entering Harrowmere's harbor. The Customs House is their local headquarters.

## Purpose

To collect harbor duties, maintain accurate records of all cargo, and ensure the harbor account balances. Every ship that comes into the harbor pays the salt tithe, weighed against its cargo and recorded in the Salt Ledger under the seal of the assessor who cleared it.

## Power Base

- Legal monopoly on cargo assessment and taxation
- The Salt Ledger — the official record of all harbor duties paid
- The heron seal of the Master Assessor, pressed in grey wax
- Physical control of the Customs House, its ledgers, and its cellars of dockets

## Members

- **Ilse Varga** — Master Assessor. Has cleared more cargo than anyone in the building. Wears the heron ring.
- **Wren Adair** — Junior clerk. Three years in post. Currently conducting the year-end reconciliation.
- Various weighers on the quay who write the initial dockets
- Unnamed clerks who have sat at the tithe desk over the past nineteen years

## Conflicts

- **Internal:** The nineteen-year fraud involving the _Margery Lane_. The harbor account balances, but the money is being paid for a ship that does not exist — or for something that does, under a dead ship's name.
- **External:** Tension with the Tidewardens, who are older than the Customs House and do not much care for it.
