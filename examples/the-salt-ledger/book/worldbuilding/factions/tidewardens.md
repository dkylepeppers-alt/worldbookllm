# Tidewardens

The harbor pilots of Harrowmere — the men and women who bring ships in past the Teeth. Older than the Customs House and do not much care for it.

## Purpose

To guide ships safely through the treacherous passage at the harbor mouth. Nobody brings a ship into Harrowmere without a Tideward at the wheel.

## Power Base

- Essential, irreplaceable expertise in navigating the Teeth
- Older than the Customs House — their authority predates formal government
- Their boats are kept at berth nine, at the far end of the north quay past the fish market
- Knowledge of the harbor that no outsider can match

## Members

- Unnamed men and women who pilot ships past the Teeth
- Their boats are moored at berth nine

## Conflicts

- **External:** Do not much care for the Customs House, which is younger and more bureaucratic
- **Mystery:** Berth nine is where their boats are kept, and nobody has ever brought a ship in to berth nine — yet it is connected to the _Margery Lane_ docket with the message "Tell O. the lamp is lit."
