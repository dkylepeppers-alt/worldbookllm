# Salt Ledger

The official record of all salt tithes paid at Harrowmere's harbor. Every ship that comes into the harbor pays a duty weighed against its cargo, and the payment is recorded in the Salt Ledger under the seal of the assessor who cleared it.

## Description

A multi-volume ledger kept in the ledger room on the top floor of the Customs House, under the slates. The pages record ship names, cargo types, tithe amounts, dates, and the clerk who copied the entry. Each entry is sealed with a pressed circle of grey wax containing the assessor's mark — for Ilse Varga, a heron standing on one leg.

## Function

- Legal record of harbor taxation
- Tool for year-end reconciliation: someone must walk the ledger back through every quarter, page by page, and make sure what the Customs House was paid matches what it wrote down
- In the story, it is also the evidence of a nineteen-year fraud: the _Margery Lane_ appears in every quarter, though the ship sank nineteen years ago

## History

- Kept for generations, with volumes going back decades on the shelf in the ledger room
- Every clerk who has worked the tithe desk in nineteen years has copied the _Margery Lane_ entry, including Wren Adair two years ago
- The harbor account always balances

## Current State

In Wren Adair's hands during the year-end reconciliation. She has copied the _Margery Lane_ entries onto a loose sheet and folded it into her coat. The volumes have been returned to the shelf in the order she found them. The anomaly is still in the ledger, still sealed, still balancing — until Wren decides what to do with what she knows.
