# Margery Lane Docket

The autumn quarter tithe docket for the _Margery Lane_, found by Wren Adair in the Customs House cellars. Written on cheap grey paper in a weigher's careful capitals.

## Description

- **Cargo:** Copper ingot, forty hundredweight
- **Berth:** Nine
- **Master's signature:** A single looping "Cask"
- **Seal:** The heron seal of Master Assessor Ilse Varga, crisp and fresh
- **Hidden message:** In pencil, in a different hand: "Tell O. the lamp is lit"

## Significance

This docket is physical evidence that the _Margery Lane_ — a ship supposedly lost nineteen years ago — is still active in the harbor records. The message suggests a signal system tied to berth nine, where the Tidewardens keep their boats. Wren is holding it when Ilse Varga confronts her in the cellar.
