# Margery Lane

A brigantine, two masts, with a hold full of copper. Nineteen years ago she went out past the Teeth in a squall with eleven men aboard, and the Teeth kept her. Everyone in Harrowmere knows her name. There is a plaque about it on the harbor wall.

## Description

A brigantine — a two-masted sailing vessel. At the time of her loss, her hold was full of copper ingots. She had a master: O. Cask. Eleven men were aboard when she went out into the squall.

## Function

In life, a working merchant vessel carrying copper. In the story's present, her name serves as the cover for a continuing financial arrangement: every quarter for nineteen years, tithe dockets have been filed in her name, and the salt tithe has been paid into the harbor account. The _Margery Lane_ is either a ghost ship that still pays its taxes, or a code name for something else that comes into berth nine under the Tidewardens' watch.

## History

- **Nineteen years ago:** Lost on the Teeth in a squall. Officially, she sank with all hands.
- **Nineteen years ago–present:** Despite being at the bottom of the sea, the _Margery Lane_ has paid its salt tithe every quarter without a break. Every entry carries the heron seal of Master Assessor Ilse Varga.
- **Present:** Wren Adair discovers the anomaly during year-end reconciliation. The docket for the autumn quarter bears the master's signature "Cask" and a pencilled message: "Tell O. the lamp is lit." Berth nine is noted.

## Current State

Officially: at the bottom of the sea past the Teeth, a memorial plaque on the harbor wall her only marker.

Unofficially: her name is still active in the Salt Ledger. A ship — or something — is using her identity to pay harbor duties and move cargo through berth nine, with the Tidewardens' knowledge and Ilse Varga's seal.
