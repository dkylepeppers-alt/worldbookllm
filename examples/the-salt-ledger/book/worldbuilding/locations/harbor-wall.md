# Harbor Wall

The harbor wall of Harrowmere bears a plaque commemorating the _Margery Lane_, lost on the Teeth nineteen years ago with all hands.

## Description

A wall along the harbor, notable for the memorial plaque to the _Margery Lane_. Wren Adair's father took her to see it when she was small, and told her that this was what the sea did to people who were in a hurry.

## Notable Features

- **Margery Lane plaque:** Commemorates the brigantine lost nineteen years ago with eleven men aboard and a hold full of copper

## Current State

Standing. The plaque is still there, marking a ship that officially no longer exists — yet still pays its salt tithe every quarter.
