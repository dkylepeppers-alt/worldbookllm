# Berth Nine

A berth at the far end of the north quay in Harrowmere harbor, past the fish market. The Tidewardens keep their boats here. Nobody, as far as Wren Adair knows, has ever brought a ship in to berth nine — yet the _Margery Lane_'s tithe docket lists it as the landing point.

## Description

The last berth on the north quay, beyond the fish market and the usual traffic of the harbor. It is where the Tidewardens — the harbor pilots who bring ships past the Teeth — moor their own boats. Its presence on the _Margery Lane_ docket, with the message "Tell O. the lamp is lit," suggests it is part of a covert arrangement that has run for nineteen years.

## Notable Features

- **Tidewarden boats:** Moored here, not commercial vessels
- **The signal:** The pencilled message on the docket suggests a lamp is lit here as a signal

## Current State

Mysterious. Officially a Tidewarden berth. Unofficially, possibly the landing point for whatever moves under the name of the dead _Margery Lane_.
