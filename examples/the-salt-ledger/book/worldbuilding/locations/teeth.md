# Teeth

The Teeth are the treacherous reef or passage at the mouth of Harrowmere's harbor. They have kept ships for as long as the town has stood — patiently, and with no intention of giving anything back.

## Description

A natural hazard at the harbor entrance, presumably a reef or shoal of rocks that tear hulls in bad weather. In a squall, they are deadly: the _Margery Lane_ went out past the Teeth and did not come back. The Tidewardens are the only ones who can reliably bring ships in past them.

## History

The Teeth have claimed ships for generations. The most famous loss is the _Margery Lane_, a brigantine that went out in a squall nineteen years ago with eleven men aboard and a hold full of copper. There is a plaque about it on the harbor wall.

## Culture & Customs

- No ship enters Harrowmere without a Tideward at the wheel — the pilots know the safe passage through the Teeth
- The harbor plaque serves as a warning and a memorial
- Local wisdom: "This was what the sea did to people who were in a hurry"

## Notable Features

- **Harbor plaque:** Commemorates the _Margery Lane_ and her crew
- **The passage itself:** Navigable only by experienced Tidewardens

## Current State

Still active, still dangerous. The _Margery Lane_ is at the bottom of the sea past the Teeth — or so the official story says.
