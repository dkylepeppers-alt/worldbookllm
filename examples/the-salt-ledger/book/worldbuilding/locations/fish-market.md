# Fish Market

The fish market of Harrowmere sits along the north quay, between the main harbor and berth nine at the far end.

## Description

A working fish market on the harbor, where the catch is landed and sold. It lies along the north quay, past which berth nine sits at the quay's far end. The market is part of the everyday life of the port, a place of salt cod and harbor noise.

## Current State

Active. Mentioned as a landmark on the route to berth nine.
