# North Quay

The north quay of Harrowmere harbor stretches along the water, running past the fish market to berth nine at its far end.

## Description

A working quay where ships tie up and cargo moves. The fish market sits along it, and beyond that lies berth nine, where the Tidewardens keep their boats. The quay is part of the everyday harbor life of Harrowmere, though berth nine at its far end is unusual — nobody, as far as Wren Adair knows, has ever brought a ship in there.

## Notable Features

- **Fish market:** Sits along the north quay
- **Berth nine:** At the far end, past the fish market, where the Tidewardens moor their boats

## Current State

Active and in use. Connected to the mystery of the _Margery Lane_ through the tithe docket that lists berth nine as the landing point.
