# clue-wrens-own-handwriting-on-docket

## Clue

Among the _Margery Lane_ entries, Wren finds one in her own handwriting from two years ago — her slanted sevens, her habit of crossing the double-l in "full" as if it were a t. She does not remember writing it. Someone brought her a docket with the heron seal already on it, and she copied it into the ledger along with forty others, thinking nothing of it.

## Planting Plan

Planted in Chapter 1 when Wren examines the ledgers and recognizes her own hand in an entry for the ghost ship.

## Payoff Plan

Wren's unwitting participation implicates her in the fraud and raises the stakes personally. It also demonstrates how the system has operated: clerks copy what they are given without question, and the fraud hides in plain sight.
