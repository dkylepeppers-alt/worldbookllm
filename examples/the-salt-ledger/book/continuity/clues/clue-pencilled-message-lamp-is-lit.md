# clue-pencilled-message-lamp-is-lit

## Clue

On the _Margery Lane_'s docket, underneath the weigher's entry and the heron seal, someone has written in pencil: "Tell O. the lamp is lit." This is not standard tithe documentation. "O." likely refers to O. Cask, the master listed on the docket — the same name as the captain who went down with the _Margery Lane_ nineteen years ago.

## Planting Plan

Planted in Chapter 1 when Wren finds the docket in the cellar and reads the pencilled line.

## Payoff Plan

The message suggests an active signal or communication system. Who wrote it, who was meant to deliver it, and what "the lamp is lit" signifies remains to be discovered.
