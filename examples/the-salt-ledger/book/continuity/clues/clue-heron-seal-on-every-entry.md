# clue-heron-seal-on-every-entry

## Clue

Every entry for the _Margery Lane_ across nineteen years carries the same seal: a pressed circle of grey wax with a heron standing on one leg inside it. Wren recognizes this as the seal on Master Assessor Ilse Varga's ring, which she wears on her right hand.

## Planting Plan

Planted in Chapter 1 when Wren traces the entries back through multiple ledgers and notices the identical seal on every quarter's payment.

## Payoff Plan

The heron seal connects the ghost ship's payments directly to Varga. Whether she is the sole architect of the fraud or part of a larger system remains to be discovered.
