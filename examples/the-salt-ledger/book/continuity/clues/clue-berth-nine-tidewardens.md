# clue-berth-nine-tidewardens

## Clue

The _Margery Lane_'s docket lists berth nine as its mooring. Berth nine is at the far end of the north quay, past the fish market, where the Tidewardens keep their boats. Nobody brings a ship into Harrowmere without a Tideward at the wheel, and nobody, as far as Wren knows, has ever brought one in to berth nine.

## Planting Plan

Planted in Chapter 1 when Wren reads the docket in the cellar. The berth number is recorded in the weigher's careful capitals alongside the cargo and master's signature.

## Payoff Plan

The significance of berth nine — why a ghost ship would be registered at a Tideward berth, and what the Tidewardens' involvement might be — remains to be discovered.
