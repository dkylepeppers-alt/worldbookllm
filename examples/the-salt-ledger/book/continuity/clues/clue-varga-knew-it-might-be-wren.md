# clue-varga-knew-it-might-be-wren

## Clue

When Varga finds Wren in the cellar, she says: "I wondered who it would be... I thought, once, that it might be you." This reveals that Varga has been expecting someone to discover the _Margery Lane_ entries — and that she had specifically considered Wren as a possibility.

## Planting Plan

Planted in Chapter 1 during the cellar confrontation between Wren and Varga.

## Payoff Plan

Varga's statement implies she has been watching for discovery, and that she sees something particular in Wren (perhaps her careful attention to detail, which Varga herself once encouraged). Whether this is a warning, a test, or an invitation remains to be discovered.
