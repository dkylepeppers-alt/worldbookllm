## Seed

Reached by choosing "Make me understand what the balance keeps alive" in *The Habit of Nineteen Years*.

Wren asks Varga to make her case—to explain what good the arrangement has done, what would break if it stopped, who would be harmed. This is clerical due process: before closing an account, you understand what it was meant to record. Varga's answer may be persuasive or hollow, but Wren will have heard it.
