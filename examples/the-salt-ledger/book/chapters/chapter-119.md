## Seed

Reached by choosing "Copy her confession as you would a docket" in *The Habit of Nineteen Years*.

Wren takes up pen and paper and writes down Varga's confession as a clerk would copy a docket—methodically, completely, without commentary. This creates a new kind of evidence: not the copied ledger entries, but Varga's own words in her own voice, witnessed and recorded. The act changes their relationship permanently.
