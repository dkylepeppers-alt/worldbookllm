# Wren Adair

Junior clerk at the Customs House of Harrowmere, three years in post. Sixteen when she started, now nineteen. Meticulous, patient, and observant — she likes that columns either add up or they do not, and that the reason is written down somewhere if you are willing to look.

## Appearance

Young, trying not to look it when she first arrived. Still junior enough to work the year-end reconciliation that no one else wants.

## Personality & Traits

- Methodical and thorough — she does not skim
- Patient — she does not mind solitary evening work in the ledger room
- Observant — notices her own handwriting and habits years later
- Cautious but persistent — copies evidence, puts volumes back in order, thinks before acting
- Values truth and accountability — disturbed that money is real but the ship is not

## Backstory

Grew up in Harrowmere. Her father took her to see the plaque for the _Margery Lane_ on the harbor wall when she was small, telling her "this was what the sea did to people who were in a hurry." Started at the Customs House at sixteen. Ilse Varga once told her, "You see what others skim. Don't stop." Wren kept that page in the drawer of her desk, under the spare nibs.

## Motivations & Goals

- **External want:** To understand why a ship that sank nineteen years ago is still paying the salt tithe
- **Internal need:** To prove that her careful attention matters, and that the truth is worth finding even when it is dangerous
- **Conflict:** Her loyalty to Varga as a mentor clashes with her discovery that Varga may be complicit in the fraud

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Quiet, precise, unassuming. Speaks in plain statements. Under pressure her voice comes out steadier than she expects.

> "The reconciliation. I'm behind."

> "Then you know what I found."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A junior clerk who trusts the system and admires her mentor
- **Key turning points:** Discovers the _Margery Lane_ entries; realizes her own handwriting is among them; confronts Varga in the cellar
- **Ending state:** TBD — must choose between letting the system continue or exposing what she has found
