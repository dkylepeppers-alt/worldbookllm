# O. Cask

Master of the brigantine _Margery Lane_. Listed as the ship's master in the Salt Ledger entries. Signed the tithe docket with a single looping signature: "Cask." The _Margery Lane_ was lost on the Teeth nineteen years ago with eleven men aboard — yet the tithe entries continue, still bearing his name.

Master of the _Margery Lane_, a brigantine with two masts and a hold full of copper. The ship went out past the Teeth in a squall and was lost with all hands.
