# The Weigher

An unnamed customs weigher who writes the tithe dockets on the quay. The _Margery Lane_'s autumn quarter docket was written in "a weigher's careful capitals."

## Appearance

Unknown.

## Personality & Traits

Unknown. A functionary who writes dockets in careful capitals, signs them, and brings them to the tithe desk.

## Current State

Unknown. May be complicit in the _Margery Lane_ arrangement, or may simply be one of many weighers who has written a docket without questioning it over the years.
