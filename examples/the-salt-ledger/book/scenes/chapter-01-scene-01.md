# The Discovery

## Purpose

Wren Adair discovers that the _Margery Lane_ — a ship lost nineteen years ago — has paid its salt tithe every quarter without a break, including the most recent autumn quarter seven weeks ago. Every entry carries the heron seal of Master Assessor Ilse Varga.

## Continuity Notes

- Wren is doing the year-end reconciliation, a task nobody else wants
- She is working in the ledger room on the top floor of the Customs House
- The Margery Lane entries go back nineteen years without interruption
- The heron seal appears on every entry
