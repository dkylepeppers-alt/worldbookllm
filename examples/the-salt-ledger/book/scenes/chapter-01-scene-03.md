# The Cellar

## Purpose

Wren goes to the Customs House cellars to find the original dockets. She locates the Margery Lane docket for the autumn quarter: copper ingots, forty hundredweight, berth nine, signed "Cask." Beneath, in pencil, someone has written "Tell O. the lamp is lit." Berth nine is at the far end of the north quay, where the Tidewardens keep their boats.

## Continuity Notes

- The cellar smells of brine and old paper; some boxes have swollen and split from tidal flooding
- The docket is on cheap grey paper in a weigher's careful capitals
- The heron seal is crisp and fresh
- The pencilled message is in a different hand from the docket
- Wren hears the cellar door open at the top of the stairs
