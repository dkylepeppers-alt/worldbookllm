# The Investigation

## Purpose

Wren investigates further, going back through previous volumes. She finds her own handwriting on a Margery Lane entry from two years ago. She copies the evidence and considers that the money is real and the harbor account balances — the Margery Lane is paying for something that exists under a dead ship's name.

## Continuity Notes

- Wren discovers her own slanted sevens and crossed double-l on a two-year-old entry
- She does not remember writing it, though she remembers the foggy quarter
- She copies the dates, amounts, and clerk names onto a loose sheet
- She puts the volumes back in order
- She keeps Varga's first correction note in her desk drawer downstairs
